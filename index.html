Introducing LegacyOS

The complete app for running your personal office as a team leader.

LegacyOS puts everything you're currently tracking in your head and WhatsApp groups into one place.

Members check in with a QR code, so you always know who's showing up in office. You log earnings and it builds a live leaderboard of top earners. You can organise members into training classes and track how they're growing. And if you run more than one office, you can link them and see how your whole network is doing from one place.

Every office gets its own web address, so it feels like your own system, not some generic app. Works on any phone, nothing to install.

Get started in minutes

Four simple steps to digitise your office attendance and member tracking.

1

Create your office

Go to app.legacyosapp.com and create your office. It takes about a minute.

2

Choose your identity

Pick your office name and web address. This becomes your office's private link.

3

Add your members

Add members manually or share your office link so members can register themselves.

4

Start using your office

Post your QR code in the office and members can start checking in.
